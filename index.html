Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about safety, payments, and tutoring in Ghana.

How does the MoMo Escrow payment system work?

When you book a lesson or monthly package, your funds are deposited into TutorLink's secure MoMo Escrow account. The tutor does not receive payment until after you confirm the lesson took place. This protects your money 100%.

Are tutors verified before joining?

Yes! Every tutor undergoes background verification. We verify their Ghana Card ID, National Teaching Council (NTC) license (for professional teachers), and tertiary certificates before publishing their profile.

What if my internet connection in my area is slow?

TutorLink supports both Online Video Tuition and In-Person/Home Tuition. If video calls lag, tutors offer low-bandwidth voice notes and customized worksheets via WhatsApp, or you can opt for an in-person tutor near your town!
